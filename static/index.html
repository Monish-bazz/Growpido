Give it a LinkedIn URL. It takes the profile apart into single verifiable facts, hunts each one down across independent sources, and hands back three piles: what you can publish, what needs a citation attached, and what it refuses to let through.

House rules travel with the output: no number without a source, no superlative that traces back to the subject's own press release, and nothing reaches a client until a human signs it off.

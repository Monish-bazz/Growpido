About
Div Turakhia is an Indian-born serial tech entrepreneur, self-made billionaire, and investor.

Recognized by Forbes and Hurun as the youngest Indian billionaire globally (2016–2019), he has founded, scaled, and successfully exited multiple groundbreaking ventures in online advertising, domain services, and AI-driven businesses.

A self-taught programmer, Div began coding at age 8 and consulting for major corporations by 14. His entrepreneurial journey is marked by extraordinary milestones—achieving a net worth of over $1M at 18, $100M at 23, and surpassing $1B at 33.

Over the past 25+ years, Div has founded, scaled, and exited industry-leading companies, including:

• Ai.tech (Present) – In Jan 2022, Div founded Ai.tech, a cutting-edge startup studio and holding company, dedicated to building businesses that harness AI and machine learning to drive competitive advantage. Today, Ai.tech’s portfolio companies employ over 1600 people worldwide.

• Media.net (2010–2016, 2023–Present) – Founded by Div with a $2M personal investment, he scaled Media.net into one of the world’s top five online advertising businesses. Through strategic growth and innovation, he built it into a market leader, culminating in a $900M acquisition in 2016—the third-largest ad-tech deal of its time. In 2023, Div re-acquired 100% of Media.net's SSP business.

• Skenzo (2005–2008) – Founded by Div at age 23 with just $20K, Skenzo grew into a domain monetization leader, reaching a $200M+ valuation before a successful exit in 2008.

• Directi (1998–2014) – Co-founded at age 16 with just $500, Directi expanded into a global web services powerhouse, culminating in a $160M exit in 2014.

• Radix (2012–Present) – Co-founded with significant personal investments to acquire top-tier TLDs, Radix has since grown into one of the largest new domain registries globally, remaining fully co-owned by the Turakhia brothers.

From pioneering internet ventures to driving AI innovation, Div continues to shape industries through cutting-edge technology and entrepreneurship.
